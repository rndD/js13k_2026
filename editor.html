Click to place/add points. In Wall mode, click adds polyline points - press Enter or the Finish button to commit the wall (a gap between two walls is a drain). In Select mode, clicking a wall (a vertex or its line) selects the whole wall first, so a wall made of many points can be dragged as one piece; click again on one of that wall's own vertices to drill in and reshape just that point. Delete removes the selected point/element/wall, Duplicate clones it with a small offset, Flip mirrors its x position across the field's center (and its aim angle, for launch pads), Rotate turns a selected launch pad's aim by 15°. Undo/Redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z) step back and forward through edits. "Save to Browser" stores your draft in localStorage (persists across reloads of this page). "Play This Level" saves then opens the real game with this draft loaded in place of the built-in level. This tool is dev-only: run it via the Vite dev server (this page, not index.html) - `vite build` only ever processes index.html, so editor.html/leveleditor.ts are never swept into the shipped game bundle.
