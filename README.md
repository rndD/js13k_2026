# Technicolor Tilt

🌈 Made by [@rndD](https://github.com/rndD) for [js13kGames 2026](https://js13kgames.com/2026/) and its **Unicorns and Rainbows** theme.

🎮 **[Play Technicolor Tilt](https://rndd.github.io/js13k_2026/)**

## ✨ Description

Technicolor Tilt is a neon roguelike pinball boss rush packed into 13 KB.

Keep your balls alive, smash through boss armor, and pick upgrades that turn every run into a different build. Red and blue power eventually combine into a rainbow, while each boss introduces more dangerous attacks, armor, and regeneration.

* Bounce between 2 pinball tables
* Fight 5 increasingly dangerous bosses
* Mix and stack 15 upgrades into powerful builds
* Find the right build and unlock **IDLE MODE**, letting powered-up flippers take over
* Enjoy punchy procedural sound effects
* Deterministic fixed-step simulation

## 🕹️ Controls

### Touch

* Hold the upper-right corner and release to launch a ball.
* Tap or hold the bottom corners to control the flippers.
* Hold a flipper as a main ball approaches to catch and aim it, then release to shoot.
* Tap a card to pick an upgrade.

### Desktop

* `↑` or `Enter` — hold and release to launch; press to confirm an upgrade.
* `←` / `→` — control the flippers; hold as a main ball approaches to catch and aim it.
* On the upgrade screen, use `←` / `→` to choose a card and `↑` or `Enter` to take it.
